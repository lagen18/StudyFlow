# Risk Mitigation and Technical Constraints

## Highest-risk feature

Assignment reminders. Browser notifications may be blocked by the student or may not work when the app is closed. If I promise reminders at all times, students could miss deadlines when those reminders fail.

## Backup plan

1. Use the assignment dashboard as the main reminder. Show soon-due and overdue tasks when the student opens StudyFlow.
2. Offer browser notifications as an extra feature while the app is open. Explain this limit clearly in the app.
3. Test notifications both allowed and blocked. If browser notifications take too much time, launch with dashboard reminders first.

Another constraint is that automatically accessing course websites may require a login or permission. In the first version, students will enter deadlines manually.
